Touchgrass

Privacy

Effective 2026-05-12. This is the full policy in plain English. There is no longer, more legalistic version — what's here is what we do.

The short version. Touchgrass stores everything on your phone. Nothing leaves it, with two exceptions you can control: a one-time code emailed to you if you enable the commitment lock, and Google Play Billing if a paid tier returns later (V1 is free). No ads. No tracking. No analytics SDKs.

What Touchgrass can see

  • Which app is currently in the foreground — but only when it's one of the apps you put on the block list (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Snapchat, Chrome, Samsung Internet).
  • UI elements within those apps, just long enough to detect that you've opened a short-form feed.
  • The fact that you opened the Touchgrass app itself.

What Touchgrass cannot see

  • Your messages, emails, banking apps, photos, or keyboard input.
  • Any app not on your block list. Even though Android's Accessibility API could technically let us, the configuration explicitly scopes us to the supported apps only. See accessibility_service_config.xml in the source.
  • Your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera.

What Touchgrass stores

  • Your settings (which apps to block, daily pause budget, friction mode, commitment-lock preferences).
  • Block counts and timestamps for the stats screen.
  • Your email address — only if you turn on the commitment lock.

All of this is on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

When Touchgrass uses the internet

  • Commitment lock. If you turn it on, we send a 6-digit code to your email via Resend (resend.com). Resend sees the recipient address and the email body (which contains the code). Nothing else. The code expires in 5 minutes.
  • Future purchases (not in V1). If a paid tier returns later, Google Play Billing handles the transaction. We never see your payment info.
  • That's it. No analytics, no tracking SDKs, no crash reporter without an explicit opt-in disclosed here.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics SDKs. Not Firebase Analytics, not Mixpanel, not Amplitude.
  • We don't show ads. Ever.
  • We don't sell data.
  • We don't have user accounts. The email for commitment lock is stored locally; you can change or remove it any time.
  • We don't have a server that knows you exist.

Children's privacy

Touchgrass is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Verify for yourself

The whole app is open source under GPL-3.0. github.com/nikolozturkishvili1/touchgrass-android — start with app/src/main/res/xml/accessibility_service_config.xml for the strict scope, and lock/ResendEmailOtpService.kt for the only network call we make.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes materially, we'll notify you inside the app before the new policy takes effect.
